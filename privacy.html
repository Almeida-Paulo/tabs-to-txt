Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This policy describes how Tabs to txt processes tab information and TXT files. The extension requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or telemetry.

Tab information

Opening the popup reads the URLs, titles, and selection state of tabs in the current window. Tab titles are displayed for identification. Exported files contain only the URLs selected for export.

Safari requires website permissions to access tab URLs. Permissions can be managed or revoked in the browser's extension settings.

File processing and website requests

Tab information and selected TXT files are processed locally. The extension does not transmit URLs, tab titles, or file contents to project servers.

Import reads the selected TXT file. After confirmation, its valid HTTP and HTTPS URLs are opened through the browser. The destination websites receive ordinary browsing requests under their own privacy policies.

Storage and deletion

The working list is held temporarily in the extension interface. The extension maintains no separate browsing history. Exported TXT files remain in the selected download location until deleted through the operating system. The extension can be removed through the browser's extension settings.

Privacy inquiries

Privacy requests can be sent to contato.tabstotxt@midnorth.com.br. Contact details and information submitted by email are used to respond to and resolve the request.

Chrome Limited Use

Information accessed through Chrome APIs is used in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Browsing activity is used exclusively to display tab selections, export URLs, and restore saved links.
