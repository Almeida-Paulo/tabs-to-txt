Privacy Policy

Tabs to txt · November 6, 2026

The extension saves tab URLs to TXT files and opens links from a TXT file in your browser. It requires no account, includes no advertising or analytics, and sends no URLs, tab titles, or TXT files to servers operated by the project.

Export and permissions

When you open the popup, the extension reads the URLs, titles, and selection of the tabs in your current window. Titles help you identify the tabs in the interface. The TXT file contains only the URLs of the tabs you choose. On Safari, access to URLs depends on the website permissions you grant.

Import

The extension reads only the file you select, on your device. After you confirm, the browser visits the HTTP and HTTPS URLs in the file. Those sites receive ordinary browsing requests and apply their own privacy policies.

Storage and deletion

The extension holds the working list temporarily in its interface and keeps no browsing history of its own. The exported TXT stays in your chosen folder or the browser's download location until you delete it. You can revoke permissions or remove the extension in your browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contato.tabstotxt@midnorth.com.br. If you contact us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond and handle your request.

Chrome Limited Use

Our use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The extension uses browsing activity only to show, export, and restore the tabs you choose.
